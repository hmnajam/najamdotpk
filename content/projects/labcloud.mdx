## Context

I founded LabCloud in 2015 to bring diagnostic laboratory workflows into one
platform. Laboratories were working with paper registers and WhatsApp; the
product covered sample intake through report delivery and included a
patient-facing results portal.

This was operational software. The engineering problem and the commercial
problem were connected: the platform needed to fit the way laboratories
actually worked.

## My responsibility

I led development, recruited a CTO, and owned product and commercial direction.
This was a leadership role with a technical team, not a claim that I wrote
every part of the platform myself.

I also sold the product into working laboratories. That responsibility connected
customer conversations to product decisions and kept the work focused on a
usable system rather than a feature list.

## What we built

The platform brought together:

- **Sample and test workflows**, from intake through laboratory processing.
- **Report delivery**, as part of the same operational workflow.
- **A patient portal**, allowing patients to retrieve their results without
  returning to collect a printout.

The product boundary followed the laboratory workflow. The portal extended it
to patients, making report access part of the service rather than a separate
manual handoff.

## Engineering and product judgment

Leading development while owning the commercial relationship required clear
decisions about what the product needed to do for a working laboratory.
Recruiting a CTO also meant building technical leadership into the company
rather than keeping every responsibility with the founder.

The experience is relevant inside a product team: understand the users' work,
agree on the product boundary, and connect delivery decisions to operational
requirements.

## Outcome and acquisition

LabCloud was sold into **30+ operational laboratories before acquisition**.
I sold the company at the end of **2019**.

It continues as **HealthCloud**, with **70+ clients under its new owners**.
That later figure belongs to HealthCloud's operation; it is not the client
count at my exit or a claim about my current involvement.

[Visit HealthCloud](https://www.healthcloud.pk/).

## What this brings to a team

LabCloud demonstrates technical leadership alongside product ownership and
customer understanding. I have worked through the full lifecycle of a software
business: identifying a workflow, leading its implementation, supporting
adoption, and taking the company to acquisition.
