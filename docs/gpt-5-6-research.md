# GPT-5.6 review research

Research conducted 10 September 2026. Publication date supplied by Najam: 16 July 2026. Scope therefore restricted to launch-era information and reviews available by that date. Live source pages can change; no claim of archived-page verification is made.

## Author evidence

Najam has tried Sol, Terra, and Luna in Codex and OpenClaw, but has not used them much. He requested an internet review synthesis instead of an experience interview. Do not attribute outside projects, failures, costs, model preferences, or benchmarks to him. No ongoing update commitment was supplied; the later Terra pricing update was explicitly requested.

## Sources used

- OpenAI launch announcement: https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/ — model positioning, availability, original API rates ($5/$30 Sol, $2.50/$15 Terra, $1/$6 Luna). Its dated later-update banners are excluded from the July article.
- OpenAI changelog: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog — July 9 release; July 30 Terra/Luna price cuts and August 21 Sol price cut confirm that current model-page prices must not be substituted for July 16 prices.
- Simon Willison, July 9: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/ — first-hand Sol impression and 18 model/effort visual tests, costing 0.71–48.55 cents. Not a coding cost benchmark.
- LayerLens, July 14: https://layerlens.ai/blog/gpt-5-6-benchmark-review-sol-terra-luna — tier recommendations and long-context caveat. Mixed source methodology explicitly acknowledged. Avoid its unsupported universal claims and inconsistent benchmark summaries; no numeric scores reproduced.
- Early Codex discussion, July 10: https://www.reddit.com/r/codex/comments/1usmvs9/what_are_we_thinking_of_gpt_56/ — mixed first-hand coding feedback. Anecdotal, self-selected, unverified, and not a statistical average. No individual numeric claims reproduced.
